\begin{abstract}
We investigate domino tilings of regions in two and three dimensions and determine conditions for when tilings must contain specified substructures. In particular, we show that a special class of regions in the plane do not admit any tilings without a $2 \times 2$ subsquare of dominoes,  and extend this result to three-dimensional boxes.
\end{abstract}
