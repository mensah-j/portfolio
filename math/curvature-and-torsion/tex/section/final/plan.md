- Introduction
  - Purpose:
    1. To clarify the different definitions of connections.
    2. To provide a geometric picture of the invariants associated to a connection.
    3. To prove a variant of the Stokes theorem generalizing the generalized Stokes theorem and the nonabelian Stokes theorem.
    4. Show how this may be used to understand the Bianchi identities.
